Architecture

The Operational Logic

STORMCloud is the intelligence layer that connects the infrastructure, managing data flow across three zones of movement.

Zone 1: Warehousing

Indoors / Local
Utilizes Short-range BLE and Wi-Fi for precise local detection within facilities.

Zone 2: Transport

Ground Transport
Switches to Cellular networks with automatic Satellite backup for seamless transit tracking.

Zone 3: Remote

Air & Water
Relies exclusively on Satellite communication when beyond terrestrial range.

Data from all zones is fed into the central Military Cloud and supported by the StormPMC server node for 24/7 monitoring.

Direct view of specific assets and location status. Quickly search and filter lists (e.g., AssetTracker8), select Geofences to view assets relative to boundaries, and instantly correlate list data with physical map positions.
